Every game starts in the lab.

Loot Lab Studios is an independent game studio crafting stylized strategy games with personality and depth.

We keep the team small and the standards high. Every character, map and mechanic is made in-house, tested early and refined until it feels right in your hands.

  1. 01

    Prototype

    Every idea becomes something playable fast, so we find the fun before anything else.

  2. 02

    Design

    Characters, worlds and systems are shaped together, so the art and the gameplay tell the same story.

  3. 03

    Build

    Characters are modelled and textured in Blender, then brought to life in Unity through a pipeline built for a small, fast team.

  4. 04

    Polish

    Feel, readability and performance are tuned until every match plays beautifully on a phone.
